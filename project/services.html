What We Do

Inventory Services Built Around Accuracy

CountWise provides practical inventory services that help businesses understand their stock, identify discrepancies, and improve inventory control.

Our Approach

Clear Information Starts With Accurate Stock

Inventory information is only useful when it accurately represents what is physically available. CountWise focuses on structured counting, careful documentation, and practical recommendations that help businesses maintain better control of their inventory.

Services can be adapted to the size, type, and operational needs of the business. Whether a business needs a physical stock count, an inventory audit, or support organizing its stock, the objective remains the same: provide reliable information that supports better decisions.

Our Services

What CountWise Can Help With

01

Stock Counting

A structured physical count of inventory to establish the quantity of stock actually available at a specific location and time.

Suitable for:

  • Periodic inventory counts
  • Year-end or month-end stock counts
  • Warehouse stock verification
  • Stocktake preparation
02

Inventory Auditing

A review of physical inventory and available inventory records to identify differences, weaknesses, and areas requiring attention.

Areas reviewed may include:

  • Physical stock versus recorded quantities
  • Stock movement records
  • Inventory discrepancies
  • Basic inventory control procedures
03

Warehouse Organization

Practical support for organizing inventory so that stock can be located, identified, counted, and managed more efficiently.

Areas of support may include:

  • Stock location organization
  • Product identification
  • Arrangement of inventory areas
  • Improving stock visibility
04

Inventory Management Support

Practical support for businesses looking to strengthen their inventory processes and make better use of inventory information.

Support may include:

  • Inventory process reviews
  • Stock control recommendations
  • Inventory documentation support
  • Identification of process weaknesses
