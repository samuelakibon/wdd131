Why It Matters

Better Inventory Information Supports Better Decisions

When inventory records do not accurately reflect physical stock, businesses can face avoidable operational challenges.

  • Unexplained stock discrepancies
  • Difficulties locating inventory
  • Inaccurate inventory records
  • Unnecessary stock shortages or excesses
  • Reduced confidence in inventory information
