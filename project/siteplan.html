Site Plan

Site Name:

CountWise Inventory Services

Site Purpose:

The CountWise Inventory Services website provides businesses with professional inventory management solutions, including stock counting, inventory audits, warehouse organization, and inventory consulting. The site allows potential clients to learn about the company's services, understand the benefits of accurate inventory management, request a service quote, and contact the company for inquiries or support.

Scenarios:

🎯 How can CountWise Inventory Services help my business reduce inventory errors and improve stock accuracy?

🎯 What inventory services do you offer, and which one is best suited for my business?

🎯 How can I request a stock count or inventory audit, and what information do I need to provide?

Color Schema:

Navy Blue (#1F3A5F): Used for the header, navigation bar, headings, and footer. This color represents trust, professionalism, and reliability.

Orange (#F39C12): Used for buttons, links, icons, hover effects, and other accent elements. It draws attention to important actions such as requesting a quote or contacting the company.

White (#FFFFFF): Used as the primary background color to create a clean, organized, and easy-to-read layout.

Typography:

Poppins: Used for headings, navigation links, and buttons. This font gives the website a modern and professional appearance that is suitable for a business-focused inventory service.

Roboto: Used for body text, paragraphs, and form labels. This font is easy to read on both mobile and desktop devices and helps maintain a clean and organized layout.
